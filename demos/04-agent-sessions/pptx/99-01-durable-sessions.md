# Sessions are durable objects

## Content

- The **window** runs sessions side by side, the **host** owns their state
- **Remote hosts** give every session the same toolchain
- `/chronicle` and `/troubleshoot` turn history into **answers**

## Notes

Close by running /chronicle standup live: the room sees the day's sessions come back as a summary with links.
