# The agent outside the editor

## Content

- Ask and automate from the terminal: **`copilot`** and **`copilot -p`**
- Put a recurring job on a schedule: **`gh aw`**
- Embed the runtime in your product: the **SDK**
- Check what reached the session: **`/env`**

## Notes
