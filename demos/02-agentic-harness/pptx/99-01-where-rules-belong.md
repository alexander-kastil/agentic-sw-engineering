# Where a rule belongs

## Content

- True for everyone, any harness: **AGENTS.md**
- Scoped to one stack: **`.instructions.md`** with `applyTo`
- A procedure a colleague would walk you through: **skill**
- Output that must be exact: **skill with a script**
- Must be enforced or audited: **hook**

## Notes

Close by asking which of their current always-on rules should move down a layer.
