# The GitHub Copilot harness

## Content

- Nine layers around the model: **instructions**, prompts, MCP, skills, agents, plugins, memory, hooks, interop
- Each layer answers one question: what Copilot knows, what it can do, or what it may do
- The goal: the **right context at the right moment**, and nothing more

## Notes

A harness does not do the work for you; it makes the work safe and repeatable. The model does the work, the harness decides what it sees and what it is allowed to touch.
