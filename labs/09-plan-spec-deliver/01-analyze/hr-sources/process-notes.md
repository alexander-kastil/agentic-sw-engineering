# Process Notes: Filling an Open Position

Recorded by Anna Schmidt (Restaurant Manager) during one week of hiring, 14 to 18 September 2026.

| Day | Step | Tool | Minutes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | A chef hands in notice; I add a row to the Vacancies sheet with title, skills and languages | Excel | 5 |
| Mon | I ask the HR assistant in Copilot Chat to list all employees, then copy their skills into the Staff Copy sheet so I can filter them | Copilot Chat, Excel | 20 |
| Mon | I filter Staff Copy by skill and type matching names into the Internal Candidates column | Excel | 15 |
| Tue | Two names in Staff Copy were out of date; I ask the HR assistant again and fix the rows by hand | Copilot Chat, Excel | 15 |
| Tue | I email the owner of the vacancy with the candidate list pasted from Excel | Outlook | 10 |
| Wed | A second vacancy for the bar; same steps: new row, re-copy staff, filter, type names | Excel, Copilot Chat | 40 |
| Thu | The owner moves an internal candidate into the role; I update the employee's role through the HR assistant and set the vacancy Status to Filled | Copilot Chat, Excel | 10 |
| Fri | I send the weekly open-positions summary, counting open rows and reading Days Open | Excel, Outlook | 15 |

What costs the most: re-copying staff into Excel every time, because the HR assistant knows the employees but not the vacancies. Days Open is the only number management asks for.
